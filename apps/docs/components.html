Components

Everything in one place. Each page documents every prop and customization option.

Banner

Announcement bar with transitions, dismiss, auto-hide & action slots.

Button

4 variants, 5 sizes, 5 colors, icons, links, loading & block.

Card

Flexible container with media, header, body, footer & avatar slots.

Input

5 variants, leading/trailing icons, password toggle, validation & loading.

Checkbox

6 radius levels, sizes, colors, indeterminate & block/card mode.

Date Picker

7 modes, 6 formats, presets, week numbers, disabled dates & ranges.

Alert

4 variants, 5 colors, auto icons, dismissible & sizes.

Accordion

Single or multi-open, 4 variants, leading icons & disabled items.

Spinner

5 sizes, inherits color, used by every loading state.

Icon

Any Tabler icon, 5 sizes, color & aria labels.

Form Field

Label, required marker, hint, error & valid feedback wrapper.
